Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Desh Ki Nabz Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data is accessed

The app uses Google's OAuth with the scope youtube.upload. It can only upload videos to the YouTube channel of the account that authorised it. It does not read, collect or store personal data of any other person.

How data is stored

The OAuth credentials are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub Actions settings. They are not shared with any third party and are not published anywhere.

Sharing

No user data is sold, shared or disclosed. Videos are uploaded only to the owner's channel.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/rishuwrites-coder/hindi_news_bot/issues.
